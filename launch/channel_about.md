# Channel About — 2147 News Network

**2147 News Network** is a fictional future-news broadcast from the year 2147.

We cover the major stories shaping Earth, Luna, Mars, and the Outer Belt: space politics, climate adaptation, AI rights, lunar labor, future markets, science missions, and the societies emerging beyond Earth.

This channel is a speculative worldbuilding project presented in the style of a serious international news network.

## Disclaimer

This is a fictional speculative news broadcast set in the year 2147. Created for entertainment, storytelling, and worldbuilding purposes.
