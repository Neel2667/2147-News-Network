# Master Plan — 2147 News Network

## Vision

Build a fictional future-news production system for YouTube. Each episode feels like a serious news broadcast from the year 2147, covering Earth, Mars, Luna, AI rights, climate, space politics, science, economy, and culture.

## Why It Can Work

The concept has strong repeatability:
- every episode can cover several headlines
- ongoing fictional events create continuity
- futuristic visuals can be generated with web technology
- viewers may return to follow the fictional timeline

## Audience

Primary:
- sci-fi fans
- technology enthusiasts
- futurism viewers
- documentary/news-style YouTube viewers

Secondary:
- students interested in future scenarios
- worldbuilding and speculative fiction communities
- space and AI communities

## Content Promise

Every video answers:

> What would the news look like if we were watching it in 2147?

## Episode Format

1. Opening transmission
2. Top headlines
3. Main story
4. Data/context segment
5. Quote/expert segment
6. Secondary stories
7. Closing transmission

## Recommended Runtime

MVP videos: 5–8 minutes  
Standard videos: 8–12 minutes  
Special reports: 20–40 minutes

## Fictional But Plausible

Stories should be imaginative but grounded in plausible extensions of current trends:
- climate adaptation
- off-world settlements
- AI governance
- robotics and labor
- biotechnology
- energy systems
- space economy
- privacy and identity

## Tone

Serious, cinematic, immersive, and believable. Avoid comedy unless making a special satirical segment.
