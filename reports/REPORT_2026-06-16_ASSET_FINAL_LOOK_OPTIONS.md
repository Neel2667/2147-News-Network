## Options Included

### Option A — Clean Global News Style

Footage dominates. Broadcast graphics are clean, serious and minimal.

Best for:

- politics
- breaking reports
- serious B-roll
- archive/present-day style coverage

### Option B — Studio + Video Wall

Anchor/studio scene with a large video wall.

Best for:

- anchor intros
- climate/science/legal stories
- over-the-shoulder reports

### Option C — Apple-Clean Premium Report

Minimal, spacious, premium map/explainer design.

Best for:

- explainers
- maps
- documentary-style context segments

### Option D — Data-Heavy Bloomberg Style

Dense metrics and dashboard style.

Best for:

- finance
- elections
- legal case boards
- science telemetry

## Important

No AI-generated images or videos. Footage/map layers are procedural placeholders for approval only. They will later be replaced with licensed stock footage, public-domain assets, or procedural maps.
