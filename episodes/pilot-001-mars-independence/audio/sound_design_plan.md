# Sound Design Plan — Pilot Episode 2147-001

Episode: **Mars Votes for Independence From Earth**  
Target style: premium international news + Apple-keynote polish + restrained futuristic newsroom.

## Audio Philosophy

The sound must feel expensive, calm, serious, and credible. Avoid cheap sci-fi sounds, loud whooshes, overused glitch effects, robotic voices, and trailer-style hype.

The audio should support the illusion of a real future broadcast:

- clean anchor voice
- subtle newsroom bed
- premium transition stings
- restrained UI/data sounds
- soft low-frequency tension for political stakes
- no clutter under important narration

## Reference Feeling

Think:

```text
BBC/CNN/Al Jazeera seriousness
+ Bloomberg market clarity
+ NASA mission-control restraint
+ Apple event sound polish
```

Not:

```text
TikTok AI voice
cheap cyberpunk trailer
loud glitch pack
hacker UI beeps everywhere
```

## Voiceover Direction

Anchor: **Anaya Rao**

Voice qualities:

- calm
- precise
- international-news tone
- medium pace
- confident, not dramatic
- serious but not cold
- slight warmth during human-impact lines

Delivery speed:

```text
135–150 words per minute
```

For dense data sections:

```text
120–135 words per minute
```

## Music Bed

Use a subtle continuous bed under most of the episode.

Characteristics:

- low pulse
- soft synth texture
- no melody that distracts
- warm sub bass, very low volume
- gentle tension for Mars political stakes

Approximate loudness:

```text
Voice: -14 to -11 LUFS momentary focus
Music bed under voice: -30 to -24 LUFS
SFX: never louder than voice unless during intro/outro stings
Final integrated target: around -14 LUFS for YouTube
True peak: below -1.0 dBTP
```

## SFX Palette

Allowed sounds:

- soft transmission start
- elegant low boom
- subtle glass UI tap
- light data tick
- calm orbital whoosh
- restrained lower-third reveal
- soft ticker pulse
- clean transition shimmer

Avoid:

- sirens unless truly breaking-news
- aggressive glitches
- stock sci-fi lasers
- loud risers
- robot voice effects
- constant beeping

## Scene-by-Scene Sound Design

| Scene | Audio Treatment |
|---|---|
| Opening Transmission | Premium 2–3 sec network sting, soft orbital swell, subtle transmission lock. Music bed begins. |
| Anchor Lead-In | Music drops under voice. Gentle newsroom ambience. Lower-third reveal sound very subtle. |
| Top Headlines | Slightly faster pulse. Each card gets a soft glass tap/slide, not a loud whoosh. |
| Mars Dashboard | Data blips on bar reveals. Low Mars tension tone. Keep narration very clear. |
| Historical Context | Music becomes warmer and slightly documentary-like. Soft timeline ticks for each year. |
| Expert Quote | Reduce music. Add archive-feed waveform texture very low. Quote must be clean and intelligible. |
| Market Ripple | Add restrained financial ticker pulse. Slight amber-toned data sound. No chaotic market noise. |
| Legal Ripple | More quiet, formal tone. Soft document reveal sound. No courtroom clichés. |
| Closing Transmission | Music swells slightly. Premium end-transmission shimmer. Clean final fade. |

## Licensing Rule

All music and SFX must be licensed or self-created.

Allowed:

- paid stock music/SFX
- self-made synth sounds
- public-domain audio with verified license
- properly licensed TTS if used

Not allowed:

- unlicensed movie/news themes
- copyrighted network stings
- ripped Apple/CNN/BBC sounds
- unclear free sound effects

## Final Audio QC Checklist

Before upload:

- voice is always clear
- no SFX fights the narration
- music feels premium, not cheap
- final loudness is YouTube-safe
- intro feels powerful but not loud
- ending fades cleanly
- no harsh high frequencies
- subtitles match voiceover timing
